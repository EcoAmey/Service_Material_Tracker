Service Material Tracker
User Manual

A step-by-step guide to every role in the system, with screenshots. See the Application Overview document if you want to understand what the app is and how it's built — this one is about how to use it.

Before you start

Every page has the same toolbar at the top: a search box, a part-type filter, a date range, and a sort order — use these to narrow down a long list before you start working.

Login page — which page you land on afterward depends on your role.

1. Engineer

The Engineer's home page lists every faulty part you've logged, split into two tabs: Pending (not yet shipped out) and Dispatched (already on its way).

Engineer home page.

To dispatch material

  1. Tick the checkbox next to one or more pending items.
  2. Click Dispatch at the bottom right.
  3. Fill in the Pickup Number, LR Number, Delivery Challan Number, choose a Delivery Partner and a Destination (a warehouse), add any remarks, and click Confirm Dispatch.
Dispatch dialog.

2. Regional Warehouse

Some material is routed through a Regional Warehouse first. Its home page has five tabs: Incoming, Tested OK, Repaired, Dispatched to OEM, and Dispatched to Warehouse.

Regional Warehouse home page.

For an incoming item, you have two kinds of action

  • Simple status update (Tested OK / Repaired / Dispatched to OEM) — select the item, click the matching button, add an optional remark, and confirm.
  • Forward it on to the Main Warehouse — select the item, click Dispatch, and fill in the same shipment details an Engineer would.
Dispatch onward dialog.

3. Main Warehouse

This is the busiest page — it has a tab for every stage a part can be in on its way through the warehouse: Incoming, Pending OEM/Scrap, With Service Desk, With Electronics, Repaired from Electronics, Added to Stock, Dispatched to OEM, and Scrapped.

Main Warehouse home page.

Receiving an incoming Power Card / Control Card / RMU

Select it and click Receive. Record the date it physically arrived, whether the serial number on the part matches the paperwork, a reservation number, and (if it doesn't match) a mandatory remark explaining why.

Receive material dialog.
A Power/Control Card whose serial number starts with P, or any motor/pump, skips Service Desk and Electronics entirely — after receiving, it goes straight to a Dispatch to OEM / Scrap decision instead.

Dispatch to OEM / Scrap

For motors, pumps, and the P-serial cards just described: select the item and click the matching button. Record the inward and outward reservation numbers and an optional remark.

Dispatch to OEM dialog.

Add to Stock

Once Electronics has repaired something and sent it back: select it, click Add to Stock, enter the outward reservation number, and confirm. This is the last step in a part's journey.

Add to Stock dialog.

4. Service Desk

Two tabs: Pending Inspection and With Electronics.

Service Desk home page.

To inspect and assign to Electronics

Select one or more pending items, click Assign to Electronics, and fill in the full inspection checklist — every field here is required:

  • Problem description
  • Visual inspection done? (Yes/No)
  • MTR test (Passed/Failed)
  • Cold test (Passed/Failed)
  • Wiring (Repaired/Was OK)
  • Remark
Inspection checklist dialog.

5. Electronics

Four tabs: Pending Decision, Completed, Sent Back to Warehouse, and Scrapped.

Electronics home page.

Step 1 — Diagnose

Select a pending item and click Proceed to open the full diagnostic checklist: your name, initial observation, PCB type, PCB issue, category of failure, sub category (you can pick more than one — type to search), IGBT pin status, the component actually changed, the test machine used, the tested date, and finally a Final Remark of Repaired OK, Tested OK, or Scrapped. Submitting this moves the item to the Completed tab.

Diagnostic checklist dialog.

Step 2 — Dispose

In the Completed tab, select the item. Depending on what you picked as the Final Remark, you'll see either a Send Back to Warehouse button or a Scrap button (only one applies — it's decided by the remark you already gave it). Either way, a small dialog asks for the outward reservation number.

Send Back to Warehouse dialog.

6. Admin

The Admin Dashboard is a separate area with its own sidebar, reachable only by an Admin login. It doesn't act on individual parts — it's for oversight, user management, and reporting.

Dashboard Overview

Every material in the system, in one place, with a tab per stage across the entire pipeline (not just one role's slice of it). Filterable by part type, engineer, and date, on top of the usual search and sort.

Admin Dashboard Overview.

User Management

See every login, its role, and whether it's active. From here you can:

  • + Add User — create a new login (name, email, password, role).
  • Change Password / Change Role — per-account, via a small dialog.
  • Activate / Deactivate — one click, no dialog.
User Management page.
Add User dialog.

Reports & Analytics

Two at-a-glance charts: how many materials are sitting at each stage right now, and which components/faults come up most often (pulled from Electronics' own diagnostic notes).

Reports & Analytics page.

Material Lookup

Search by Service ID, machine number, or faulty serial number. Every match shows its full history — every stage it has passed through, who touched it, and when — from the moment it was first dispatched to wherever it is (or ended up) now.

Material Lookup results.
